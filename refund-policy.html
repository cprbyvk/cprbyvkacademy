Refund Policy

Our policy on cancellations, refunds, and course access.

Last updated: August 2026

Current status

CPR by VK Academy does not currently offer any paid courses or products — every tool on this site is free to use. This Refund Policy is published in advance and will apply from the day paid courses launch.

1. Overview

At CPR by VK Academy, we are committed to delivering high-quality educational experiences. We understand that circumstances may change, and we aim to handle all future refund and cancellation requests fairly and transparently.

Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase, once courses are available. By completing a payment, you will acknowledge and accept the terms described below.

2. Eligibility for Refund

Refund requests may be considered under the following conditions:

  • The request is raised within 7 days of the original purchase date.
  • Less than 20% of the course or program content has been accessed or consumed.
  • The request is submitted in writing to cprbyvk@gmail.com with your order details and reason.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Refund request made after 7 days of purchase.
  • More than 20% of the course or program content has been accessed.
  • Digital downloads, recorded sessions, or e-books once accessed or downloaded.
  • Live webinars, workshops, or events after attendance.
  • One-on-one mentorship sessions once the session has commenced.
  • Purchases made during promotional or discounted sales.
  • Termination of access due to breach of our Terms and Conditions.

4. Cancellation of Live Sessions

If you need to reschedule or cancel a one-on-one mentorship or live session, please notify us at least 48 hours in advance. Requests made with less than 48 hours notice will be treated as attended sessions and will not be eligible for a refund or reschedule.

5. Refund Process

Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days of approval. Refunds will be credited to the original payment method used at the time of purchase. We are not responsible for any additional delays caused by your bank or payment provider.

6. Technical Issues

If you experience a technical issue preventing access to course content, please contact us at cprbyvk@gmail.com within 48 hours of the issue occurring. We will make reasonable efforts to resolve the issue or, if unresolvable, provide an appropriate remedy.

7. Course Content Changes

CPR by VK Academy reserves the right to update, modify, or restructure course content at any time to maintain quality and relevance. Such changes do not constitute grounds for a refund.
