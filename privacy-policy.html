Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Vineshkumar R (CPR by VK Academy) ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our free tools, visit our website, or (once available) enrol in our courses.

By using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not access or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Data: Name, email address, and phone number, if you message us via WhatsApp or email.
  • Communications: Messages, enquiries, or feedback you send us.
  • Transaction Data (once course sales launch): payment records, purchase history, invoices.
  • Usage Data: General, non-identifying analytics such as which pages are visited, collected by standard hosting infrastructure.

Our free CPR tools (Calculator, Scanners, Trading Journal) do not send your uploaded data anywhere. All calculations run entirely in your own browser — the CSV files, price data, or trade entries you enter are never transmitted to or stored on any server.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests.
  • To process course enrolments and payments, once available.
  • To send transactional communications (receipts, access details).
  • To send marketing communications, only with your consent.
  • To improve our website, tools, and user experience.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Payment processors to facilitate secure transactions, once course sales launch.
  • Email / communication platforms used to reach you.
  • Legal authorities where required by law, court order, or regulatory obligation.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Transaction records, once applicable, will be retained in compliance with Indian tax regulations.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies (for example, to remember your dark/light theme preference) to enhance your browsing experience. You can disable cookies via your browser settings; some site features may not function correctly as a result.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at cprbyvk@gmail.com.
