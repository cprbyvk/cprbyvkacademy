Why keep a trading journal?

Every trade carries a reason for getting in, a reason for getting out, and something to learn from — whether it went well or not. Writing that down turns a string of trades into an actual feedback loop instead of a blur of P&L.

Use this as your trade log, P&L tracker and ROI calculator in one place, for equity, options or futures — whether you're just starting out or refining an existing edge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a trading journal?

A running record of your trades — entry, exit, reasoning, P&L, ROI, mistakes and lessons — kept for self-review and improvement.

Is this free to use?

Yes. This journal runs entirely in your browser, free, as part of the CPR by VK tool set.

Is my data stored anywhere else?

No. Everything stays in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Can I export my journal?

Yes — export your saved entries as a CSV file for spreadsheets, or a PNG snapshot to share or archive.

Does it support options and futures?

Yes — Equity, Options and Futures entries are all supported, alongside a Setup/Strategy tag for your own CPR-based setups.
